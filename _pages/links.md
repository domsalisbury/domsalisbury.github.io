Useful resources on ME/CFS:

- **[Science for ME (S4ME) forum](https://www.s4me.info/)** — a forum for discussion of ME/CFS research, advocacy and news, with a focus on scientific rigour.
- **[ME/CFS Science Blog](https://mecfsscience.org/)** — articles summarising and critiquing ME/CFS research.
- **[Broken Battery (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/c/BrokenBattery)** — videos explaining ME/CFS and the history of how it has been treated by medicine.
- **[NICE guideline NG206: ME/CFS diagnosis and management](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng206)** — the current guideline for England and Wales, published in October 2021.
- **[CrunchME](https://crunchme.org/)** — data-informed advocacy to speed up research and progress on ME/CFS, Long COVID and other infection-associated chronic illnesses.
- **[Dialogues for ME/CFS (films)](https://www.dialogues-mecfs.co.uk/videos/)** — a series of short educational films on ME/CFS, covering its symptoms, management, severe illness and the history of controversy around the condition.
